Аннотация к Вайлдштормовскому ивенту Devil's Night девятилетней давности:

*The demons and ghosts of our nightmares have found a herald in Ghoulgotha, and he's about to enter our world…with a vengeance! Facing this threat head-on is the preeminent mystic of the modern age, Jezebelle — as she challenges Ghoulgotha, aiding the champions of the WildStorm Universe along the way. Jezebelle's unforgettable debut comes as part of "Devil's Night," a storyline that runs through four WildStorm Annuals (released weekly in October), and presents Ghoulgotha's entrance and first round of sadistic tamperings with WildStorm's finest heroes.*

Ghoulgotha's terrorizing begins as he is accidentally unleashed during a Halloween séance by everyone's favorite team of Gen-Actives in GEN13 ANNUAL 2000 #1, written by Ben Raab (LEGEND OF THE HAWKMAN) with art by Kaare Andrews (GEN-ACTIVE). Unknown to our heroes, it just happens to be the precise century, year, day, and time of night ideal for Ghoulgotha's return — and the mysterious Jezebelle is ready.

Then, in WILDCATS ANNUAL 2000 #1, written by Joe Casey (WILDCATS, MR. MAJESTIC) with art by Bermejo and Jim Royal, the mercenary known as Grifter is haunted by visions of his dead brother Max. How does this fit into Ghoulgotha's evil plans? This Annual features several guest-stars — both alive and dead — that WILDCATS fans have been clamoring for!

In THE AUTHORITY ANNUAL 2000 #1, written by Casey with art by Cully Hamner (ROBIN), the dead are rising from their graves — some of them with super-powers! A human atom bomb from the Cold War has resumed his personal war with the former U.S.S.R., and a floating ghost carrier ship filled with dead Russian soldiers and super-spies is fighting back. Can the Authority stop them before somebody starts WW III?

"Devil's Night" concludes in WILDSTORM ANNUAL 2000 #1, written by Raab with art by Jeff Moy and W.C. Carani (STAR TREK: VOYAGER — ELITE FORCE, LEGIONNAIRES). As the conclusion draws near, Jezebelle calls upon the only souls she feels can truly help her cause: Deathblow Michael Cray and DIVINE RIGHT's Christie Blaze! Will the three heroes be able to thwart Ghoulgotha's bid for world domination? The stunning finale of "Devil's Night" features an unexpected sacrifice by all parties involved, setting the stage for the debut of an ongoing JEZEBELLE series in November!
