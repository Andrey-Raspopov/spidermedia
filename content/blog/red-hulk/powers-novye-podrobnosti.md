*Powers on TV now has an assigned pilot director and a showrunner, which makes it sound more and more like this is going to happen. In an interview with G4TV, Bendis revealed that, although the greenlight has not yet been given to shoot, Michael Dinner (Bionic Woman, Sons Of Anarchy) will be directing the pilot. The series' showrunner will be Kevin Falls, whose previous credits include **Journeyman**, **The West Wing**, and **Sports Night**. In addition to being a producer on those projects, Falls also wrote for those series.* [Источник](http://www.thefutoncritic.com/search.aspx?q=Powers+on+TV+now+has+an+assigned+pilot+director+and+a+showrunner%2C+which+makes+it+sound+more+and+more+like+this+is+going+to+happen.++In+an+interview+with+G4TV%2C+Bendis+revealed+that%2C+although+the+greenlight+has+not+yet+been+given+to+shoot%2C+Michael+Dinner+%28Bionic+Woman%2C+Sons+Of+Anarchy%29+will+be+directing+the+pilot.+The+series%27+showrunner+will+be+Kevin+Falls%2C+whose+previous+credits+include+Journeyman%2C+The+West+Wing%2C+and+Sports+Night.+In+addition+to+being+a+producer+on+those+projects%2C+Falls+also+wrote+for+those+series.&type=titles)
